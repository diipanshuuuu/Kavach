## What we learned

- **Checksums make rules precise:** zero false alarms on 150 docs full of look-alike numbers. But rules alone find only 43% of names and 38% of addresses, because most names don't follow a cue like "Name:" or "Dear".
- **The open-weight NER model is what makes Kavach work:** recall goes from 68.9% to 97.3% with no loss in precision, at ~60 ms per document on a laptop CPU.
- **The local LLM (Qwen3-4B) finds the last few items but over-flags:** recall 99.3%, precision drops to 86%, and it takes ~4 s per document. So deep scan is an opt-in button, not the default.
